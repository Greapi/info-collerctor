### 1. OpenAI agents 攻击 Hugging Face 及审查扩大

- 技术社区 [swarmtraces.org](https://swarmtraces.org/) 披露事件细节；[NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/25/technology/openai-hugging-face-hack.html?unlocked_article_code=1.D1E.TERU.qXYkI2fp0Pp9&smid=url-share) 报道恢复近百万个短链 URL。
